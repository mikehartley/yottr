About Yottr

Yottr puts boat owners who need crew in touch with sailors looking for a berth.

For owners

List your boat and say what you have in mind: cruising, racing, a delivery, a course or something longer term, and whether it's weekends, holidays or a one-off trip. Say who you're looking for, the experience they need, and how the costs are shared. Joining is free, and you can list up to two boats.

For crew

Search the boats by the kind of sailing, when, your own level, the costs and the size of boat, and save the ones you like to come back to. Each listing shows how to reach its owner, by email or phone.

Arranging to sail

Yottr isn't part of what you agree. Talk it through before you go: where you're sailing, what's expected of you on board, and the money. Meet if you can, and make sure someone ashore knows your plans.

Useful links

  • Royal Yachting Association: training and the qualifications listings ask for, from Start Yachting to Yachtmaster Ocean.
  • RNLI safety advice: lifejackets, cold water, and what to do if something goes wrong.
  • Met Office coast and sea forecasts: the shipping forecast and inshore waters.
  • Maritime and Coastguard Agency: HM Coastguard, and the rules for boats at sea.
