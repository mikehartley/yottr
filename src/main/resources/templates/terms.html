Terms

These are a placeholder. The full terms will be here before Yottr opens to the public.

In short, for now:

  • Yottr puts owners and crew in touch. It isn't part of any arrangement to sail, and doesn't check boats, owners, crew or qualifications.
  • Only list a boat you're entitled to crew, and only add pictures you took yourself or have permission to use.
  • Listings and details must be accurate, and nothing in them may be offensive or unlawful. Administrators may take down a listing or a picture, or close an account.
