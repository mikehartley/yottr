Questions

This page will grow as people ask. Until then, these are the ones we expect.

Does it cost anything?

No. Joining, listing a boat and searching are all free.

How do I get in touch with an owner?

Each listing shows its owner's email address and phone number to signed-in members. Yottr doesn't pass messages on.

How many boats can I list?

Two to begin with. An administrator can raise the limit.

My listing has disappeared from the search

It may be suspended: look on My Listings. An administrator may also have taken it down, in which case it says so there.
