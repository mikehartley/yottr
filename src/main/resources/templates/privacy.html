Privacy

This is a placeholder. The full privacy policy will be here before Yottr opens to the public.

In short, for now:

  • Yottr keeps the details you give when you join and the listings you post.
  • Other signed-in members can see your listings, with your email address and phone number, so that crew can get in touch. Your address and postcode aren't shown to them.
  • Deleting your account, from My Details, deletes your details and your listings.
  • Yottr uses a cookie to keep you signed in, and no others.
